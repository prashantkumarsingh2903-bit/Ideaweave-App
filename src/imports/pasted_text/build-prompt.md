# BUILD PROMPT — “IDEAWEAVE”

## An AI-powered human idea incubation & collaboration platform

You are a senior product designer, UX researcher, interaction designer, and full-stack engineer.

Build a polished, functional web application called **IdeaWeave**.

IdeaWeave is NOT another social-media feed, Notion clone, Discord alternative, or portfolio platform.

It is a new category of product:

> **A private-to-public idea incubation network where unfinished ideas can mature, attract the right people, receive constructive feedback, evolve through versions, and eventually become real projects.**

The product must be designed around the principle:

**Capture → Incubate → Discover → Match → Collaborate → Evolve → Execute**

---

# 1. RESEARCH FOUNDATION

Use the following research insights as the foundation of the product.

Do NOT design features simply because they sound impressive.

Every major feature should solve a demonstrated behavioral problem.

## Insight 1 — The “Idea Graveyard”

People currently capture ideas across:

* Apple/Google Notes
* WhatsApp self-chats
* Notion
* Google Docs
* Figma
* Discord
* paper/sketchbooks
* memory

The problem is not merely capturing ideas.

The problem is that ideas are rarely revisited, connected, developed, or converted into something actionable.

Research indicates that ideas can become forgotten because there is no structured progression from:

**spark → exploration → feedback → refinement → project**

Therefore:

### Product implication

IdeaWeave must treat an idea as a **living object**, not a static post.

Every idea should have a lifecycle.

Example:

SPARK
↓
INCUBATING
↓
EXPLORING
↓
SEEKING FEEDBACK
↓
SEEKING COLLABORATORS
↓
PROTOTYPING
↓
ACTIVE PROJECT
↓
COMPLETED / ARCHIVED

# 2. INSIGHT — PEOPLE ARE AFRAID TO SHARE UNFINISHED IDEAS

Research shows that people often wait until an idea is sufficiently developed before sharing it.

They fear:

* premature judgment
* criticism
* dismissal
* people attacking edge cases
* looking inexperienced
* exposing incomplete thinking

Therefore IdeaWeave must introduce a concept called:

## “INCUBATION MODE”

An idea can exist privately first.

Privacy states:

1. PRIVATE
2. TRUSTED CIRCLE
3. SELECTED EXPERTS
4. COMMUNITY
5. PUBLIC

Users can gradually expose an idea as their confidence increases.

The UI should visually communicate:

> “You don't have to publish the finished version.”

The platform should normalize unfinished thinking.

---

# 3. INSIGHT — USERS DON'T JUST WANT AUDIENCE

Do NOT build a conventional follower/following social network.

The research indicates that users want:

* constructive feedback
* complementary skills
* people who understand their domain
* reliable collaborators
* people who remain committed
* technical/design/business expertise

Therefore the central social mechanic should NOT be:

“Follow this person.”

It should be:

> **“This person could help this idea.”**
